About This Platform

Improving health education for rural Alabama communities

Our Mission

The Rural Health Education Platform was created to make clear, simple, and trustworthy health information accessible to everyone in rural Alabama. Many people in our communities face barriers to healthcare, transportation, and reliable health education. This platform helps bridge that gap.

Who We Serve

  • Older adults
  • People managing chronic conditions
  • Families caring for loved ones
  • Community members seeking reliable health information
  • Anyone who wants to improve their health knowledge

Why This Matters

Rural communities often face higher rates of chronic disease, fewer healthcare providers, and limited access to health education. By providing easy‑to‑understand information, we empower individuals to make informed decisions about their health.

How This Platform Works

  • Simple, easy‑to‑read health guides
  • Topics chosen based on community needs
  • Information aligned with CDC and NIH recommendations
  • QR codes placed in clinics, churches, and community centers
  • Mobile‑friendly pages for quick access

Who Created This?

  • Jacksonville State University (JSU)
  • Alabama Rural Health Association
  • Local clinics, senior centers, and community organizations
