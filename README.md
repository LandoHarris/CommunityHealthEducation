Community Health Education
A free, open‑access health education platform designed to improve health literacy in rural and underserved communities. Built and maintained by Landon C. Harris, this project delivers concise, accurate, and accessible health information that can be shared in clinics, community centers, classrooms, and outreach events.

Purpose
The goal of this project is to provide clear, trustworthy, and easy‑to‑understand health education for the public. The platform focuses on:

Reducing preventable health issues through education

Supporting rural and low‑resource communities

Using technology to scale community health outreach
