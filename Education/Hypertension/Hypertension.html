Who Does Hypertension Affect?

🌱

Young Adults

Often overlooked, but about 1 in 5 are affected. Early awareness can prevent long-term heart disease.
⚖️

Middle-Aged Adults

Risk increases with lifestyle and aging. Many are diagnosed, but control remains a challenge.
🛡️

Older Adults

Most affected group. Blood pressure becomes harder to manage due to aging and chronic conditions.

Why Hypertension Matters

Hypertension is often called the "silent killer" because it frequently has no symptoms, yet it remains a leading cause of heart disease, stroke, and kidney problems. This makes regular blood pressure screenings critical for early detection.

The effects extend beyond individuals, impacting families, healthcare systems, and communities. Early prevention and management can significantly reduce long-term health risks and improve your overall quality of life.
