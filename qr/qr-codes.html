QR Code Deployment & Distribution Guide

About QR Codes

QR codes allow people to quickly access your health education platform from their phones without typing a URL. This is especially useful for:

  • Senior centers and community centers
  • Clinics and urgent care facilities
  • Churches and faith-based organizations
  • Schools and public buildings
  • Low-income neighborhoods
  • Community events and health fairs

Generate QR Codes

Using Free Online Tools

1. QR Code Generator

qr-code-generator.com

  • Select "URL"
  • Enter your site link
  • Download PNG (200–500px)

2. QR Code Monkey

qrcode-monkey.com

3. Google Charts API

https://chart.googleapis.com/chart?chs=300x300&chld=L|1&cht=qr&chl=https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHealthEducation/

QR Code Sizes & Print Specifications

SizeUse CaseRecommended DPI
1" x 1"Business cards, flyers300+
2" x 2"Posters, info sheets300+
4"–8"Community centers, clinics150+

Printing QR Codes

  • Use high contrast (black on white)
  • Avoid light colors or transparent backgrounds
  • Add labels like “Scan for Health Info”

QR Code Placement Strategy

Priority 1: High Traffic Areas

Priority 2: Healthcare Facilities

Priority 3: Community Gathering Places

Priority 4: Neighborhoods

  • Housing bulletin boards

Priority 5: Events
