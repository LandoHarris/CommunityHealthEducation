# QR Code Deployment & Distribution Guide ## About QR Codes QR codes (Quick Response codes) allow people to quickly access your health education platform from their phones without typing a URL. This is especially useful for: - Senior centers and community centers - Clinics and urgent care facilities - Churches and faith-based organizations - Schools and public buildings - Low-income neighborhoods - Community events and health fairs --- ## Generate QR Codes ### Using Free Online Tools 1. **QR Code Generator (qr-code-generator.com)** - Go to: https://www.qr-code-generator.com/ - Select "URL" - Enter: `https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHeatlhEducation/` - Download as PNG image - Size: Medium (200x200px) to Large (500x500px) 2. **QR Code Monkey (qrcode-monkey.com)** - Go to: https://www.qrcode-monkey.com/ - Enter your platform URL - Customize color (keep high contrast - dark on light or light on dark) - Download PNG - Add your logo or center image if desired 3. **Google Charts API** (No account needed) ``` https://chart.googleapis.com/chart?chs=300x300&chld=L|1&cht=qr&chl=https%3A%2F%2Fyourusername.github.io%2FCommunityHeatlhEducation%2F ``` --- ## QR Code Sizes & Print Specifications | Size | Use Case | Recommended DPI | |------|----------|-----------------| | Small (1" x 1") | Business cards, flyers | 300+ DPI | | Medium (2" x 2") | Posters, informational sheets | 300+ DPI | | Large (4" x 4" or 8" x 8") | Community centers, clinics | 150+ DPI | **Important:** The minimum size for reliable scanning is about 1cm x 1cm (0.4" x 0.4") --- ## Printing QR Codes ### Best Practices - **High Contrast:** Use black QR code on white background (or white on very dark) - **Do NOT use:** Light colors, transparent backgrounds, or complex designs - **Add Label:** Always include text below QR code like: - "Scan for Health Info" - "Learn About Your Health" - "Free Health Education" - Website URL or specific topic name ### Printing Options 1. **Home Printer:** Print on standard paper or cardstock 2. **Online Printing Services:** - Vistaprint.com - Shutterfly.com - Local print shops 3. **Large Format Printing:** For posters and banners --- ## QR Code Placement Strategy ### Priority 1: High Traffic Areas - Senior centers (most important for your audience) - Community centers - Churches and faith-based buildings - Libraries ### Priority 2: Healthcare Facilities - Clinics and health centers - Urgent care centers - Pharmacy waiting areas - Mental health facilities ### Priority 3: Community Gathering Places - Schools (bulletin boards) - Food banks - Transportation hubs - Restaurants and cafes - Barbershops and salons ### Priority 4: Neighborhoods - Low-income housing bulletin boards - Community gardens - Public parks - Bus stops ### Priority 5: Events - Health fairs - Community events - Farmers markets - Charity fundraisers --- ## QR Code Content Variations ### Option 1: Main Platform Direct users to homepage with all topics ``` URL: https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHeatlhEducation/ Label: "Scan for Health Education" ``` ### Option 2: Specific Topics Link directly to popular topics (make 2-3 versions) **Diabetes QR Code:** ``` URL: https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHeatlhEducation/topics/blood-glucose.html Label: "Learn About Blood Sugar & Diabetes" ``` **Blood Pressure QR Code:** ``` URL: https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHeatlhEducation/topics/blood-pressure.html Label: "Understand Your Blood Pressure" ``` **Emergency Signs QR Code:** ``` URL: https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHeatlhEducation/topics/emergency-signs.html Label: "Know the Signs of Stroke & Heart Attack - CALL 911" ``` ### Option 3: Local Resources ``` URL: https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHeatlhEducation/resources.html Label: "Find Local Health Services" ``` ### Option 4: Feedback Form ``` URL: https://yourusername.github.io/CommunityHeatlhEducation/feedback.html Label: "Share Your Feedback & Questions" ``` --- ## Designing Your QR Code Poster ### Template 1: Simple Flyer (8.5" x 11") ``` [Top: Large Text] RURAL HEALTH EDUCATION For You & Your Family [Middle: Large QR Code (4"x4")] [Bottom: Text] Free Health Information Scan with any smartphone www.yourplatform.com ``` ### Template 2: Topic-Specific (5" x 7") ``` [Top] Did You Know? High Blood Pressure Can Cause Stroke & Heart Disease [Middle: QR Code (3"x3")] [Bottom] Learn About Blood Pressure Scan Here → Available in English & Spanish* *Customize as needed for your community ``` ### Template 3: Event Poster (11" x 17") ``` [Large Header] HEALTH EDUCATION IS FREE [Multiple QR Codes in a grid - 6 codes for different topics] Each with its own label [Bottom text] Scan any code to learn more Available 24/7 on any smartphone ``` --- ## Tracking QR Code Performance Your Google Analytics will show: - Which pages are visited most often - How many visitors come from mobile devices - How long people spend on each page - Which health topics are most popular This data helps you understand what your community cares about most. --- ## Distribution Checklist - [ ] Generate QR codes for main topics - [ ] Print on high-quality paper or cardstock - [ ] Create attractive labels/posters - [ ] Laminate for durability (optional but recommended) - [ ] Contact senior centers and clinics - [ ] Ask permission before posting - [ ] Track where codes are placed - [ ] Check analytics monthly - [ ] Update as needed based on feedback --- ## Contact Information for Distribution When approaching organizations, use this template: --- **REQUEST FOR QR CODE PLACEMENT** Dear [Organization Name], The JSU Alabama Rural Health Association is launching a free, accessible health education platform specifically designed for rural Alabama communities, including seniors and people with limited internet access. We're offering high-quality QR codes that your community members can scan to access: - Health topics in plain language - Local resource guides - Emergency information - Mental health support Would you be willing to display a QR code poster at your facility? It's free, non-commercial, and designed to serve your community. Contact: [Your Name] Email: [Your Email] Phone: [Your Phone] --- ## Mobile User Considerations Your audience will be accessing primarily via smartphones, so remember: - Test QR codes on multiple phones - Ensure pages load quickly on slower connections - Large text is essential - Buttons should be thumb-friendly - Content should be scrollable on small screens - Minimize images for fast loading --- ## Accessibility Features All pages include: - Skip-to-content links for screen readers - Large, readable text (18px+ minimum) - High contrast colors - Keyboard navigation support - Mobile-responsive design - Plain language explanations --- ## Next Steps 1. Generate your first batch of QR codes (5-10) 2. Print on cardstock 3. Add professional labels 4. Test on multiple phones 5. Visit 3-5 community partners 6. Track results in Google Analytics 7. Adjust based on feedback 8. Scale up distribution --- ## Resources - **QR Code Generators:** qr-code-generator.com, qrcode-monkey.com - **Print Shops:** Vistaprint, Shutterfly, local print shops - **Lamination:** Office supply stores - **Analytics:** Google Analytics (free) --- **Remember:** The goal is to make health information as accessible as possible to people who might not otherwise find it online.
