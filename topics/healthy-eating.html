How Food Affects the Body

Food is Fuel and Medicine

What you eat affects how you feel, think, and function. Food provides energy and the building blocks your body needs to work properly.

Good nutrition can prevent disease and help you recover faster from illness.

How Digestion Works

When you eat:

  1. Your mouth: Saliva breaks down food as you chew
  2. Your stomach: Acid and enzymes continue breaking down food
  3. Your small intestine: Nutrients are absorbed into your bloodstream
  4. Your large intestine: Water is absorbed; waste becomes stool
  5. Elimination: Your body removes what it doesn't need

How Food Becomes Energy

Your body converts food into glucose (blood sugar) and uses it for energy:

  • Carbohydrates: Quickest energy source
  • Proteins: Build and repair tissues
  • Fats: Long-lasting energy and organ protection
  • Vitamins & minerals: Help your body use energy
  • Fiber: Helps digestion and blood sugar control

Blood Sugar and Energy

Fast Carbs = Quick Spike (then crash)

  • White bread, pastries, candy, sugary drinks
  • Quick rise in blood sugar
  • Quick drop → tired and hungry

Complex Carbs = Steady Energy

  • Whole grains, beans, vegetables, fruits
  • Slow rise in blood sugar
  • Steady energy
  • Stay full longer

How Food Affects Your Mood

Food influences brain chemicals that affect mood:

  • Good nutrition: Improves mood and reduces anxiety
  • Protein & healthy fats: Support brain health
  • B vitamins: Boost mood and energy
  • Sugar crashes: Can worsen mood
  • Regular meals: Prevent mood swings

Food and Sleep

  • Heavy meals: Eat 2–3 hours before bed
  • Caffeine: Avoid 6+ hours before sleep
  • Alcohol: Disrupts sleep quality
  • Water: Too much before bed interrupts sleep
  • Protein + complex carbs: Can improve sleep

Food and Inflammation

Foods That Increase Inflammation

  • Processed foods
  • Added sugars
  • Trans fats
  • Excess vegetable oils

Foods That Reduce Inflammation

  • Fatty fish
  • Berries
  • Leafy greens
  • Nuts & seeds
  • Olive oil
  • Turmeric & ginger

Food and Chronic Disease Prevention

Good nutrition can prevent or delay:

  • Type 2 diabetes
  • Heart disease
  • High blood pressure
  • Certain cancers
  • Obesity

Hydration Matters

Water is essential for:

  • Digestion
  • Temperature control
  • Joint lubrication
  • Brain function
  • Nutrient absorption
  • Waste removal

Goal: 8–10 glasses per day

Practical Tips

  • Eat regular meals
  • Choose whole foods
  • Eat colorful fruits & vegetables
  • Include protein at every meal
  • Drink water instead of sugary drinks
  • Notice how foods make you feel
  • Plan meals ahead
