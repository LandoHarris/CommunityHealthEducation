Blood Glucose & Diabetes Management

What Is Blood Glucose?

Blood glucose (blood sugar) is the amount of sugar in your blood. Your body uses it for energy. When levels are too high, it can cause serious health problems.

Healthy Blood Sugar Ranges

  • Before meals: 80–130 mg/dL
  • 1–2 hours after meals: Below 180 mg/dL
  • A1C goal: Below 7% (ask your doctor)

How to Check Your Blood Sugar

  • Wash your hands before testing
  • Use a clean lancet each time
  • Follow your doctor’s testing schedule
  • Record your numbers after each test

Signs of High Blood Sugar

  • Increased thirst
  • Frequent urination
  • Blurred vision
  • Fatigue

Signs of Low Blood Sugar

  • Shaking or sweating
  • Dizziness
  • Hunger
  • Confusion

If your blood sugar is below 70 mg/dL, eat or drink something sugary right away.

How to Manage Diabetes

  • Take medications as prescribed
  • Eat balanced meals
  • Exercise regularly
  • Check blood sugar daily
  • Keep regular doctor appointments

When to Call Your Doctor

  • Your blood sugar stays above 300 mg/dL
  • You have frequent low blood sugar episodes
  • You feel very sick or dehydrated
  • Your vision suddenly changes
