Tracking Vitals at Home

Why Track Your Vitals?

Tracking vital signs at home helps:

  • Monitor your health between doctor visits
  • Catch problems early
  • See if treatments are working
  • Provide information to your doctor
  • Manage chronic conditions
  • Identify patterns and triggers

What Are Vital Signs?

Vital signs show how your body is working:

  • Blood pressure
  • Heart rate (pulse)
  • Temperature
  • Breathing rate
  • Blood sugar (glucose)
  • Weight

Blood Pressure

How to Check at Home

  • Use a home blood pressure monitor
  • Sit quietly for 5 minutes before measuring
  • Rest arm at heart level
  • Place cuff on bare upper arm
  • Check at the same time daily
  • Take 2–3 readings for accuracy

What to Record

  • Date and time
  • Systolic/diastolic numbers
  • Arm used
  • How you felt

Heart Rate (Pulse)

Normal resting heart rate: 60–100 bpm

How to Check

  1. Find pulse on wrist or neck
  2. Use two fingers
  3. Count beats for 60 seconds
  4. Or count 15 seconds × 4

What It Means

  • Below 60: Ask your doctor
  • 60–100: Normal
  • Above 100: May indicate illness or stress

Temperature

Normal: 98.6°F (37°C)

How to Check

  • Digital thermometer under tongue
  • Ear thermometer
  • Forehead (temporal) scanner
  • Use same method each time

Temperature Guide

  • Below 95°F: Too low — call doctor
  • 99–100.4°F: Mild fever
  • 100.4°F+: Fever — call doctor
  • 103°F+: Emergency

Blood Sugar (Glucose)

If you have diabetes, your doctor will tell you how often to check.

How to Check

  1. Use glucose meter
  2. Wash hands
  3. Prick finger
  4. Apply blood to strip
  5. Read result

Target Ranges

  • Fasting: 70–130 mg/dL
  • After meals: Under 180 mg/dL

Weight

How to Track

  • Weigh at same time daily
  • Use same scale
  • Wear similar clothing
  • Track weekly trends

Home Monitoring Devices

When to Contact Your Doctor

  • Readings are consistently high or low
  • You see sudden changes
  • You have symptoms with abnormal readings
  • You’re unsure how to use your device
  • You want help understanding your numbers
