Fall Risk Prevention

Why Falls Matter

Falls are a major cause of injury for older adults, especially in rural communities. Many falls can be prevented with simple steps.

Common Causes of Falls

  • Poor lighting
  • Loose rugs or clutter
  • Weak muscles or balance problems
  • Medications that cause dizziness
  • Vision problems
  • Unsafe footwear

How to Prevent Falls at Home

  • Remove loose rugs and clutter
  • Install grab bars in bathrooms
  • Use non-slip mats
  • Improve lighting in hallways and stairs
  • Keep frequently used items within easy reach

Strength & Balance Exercises

  • Standing on one foot
  • Heel-to-toe walking
  • Chair stands
  • Light weight training
  • Tai chi

Medication Safety

Some medications can cause dizziness or drowsiness. Talk to your doctor if you notice:

  • Feeling lightheaded
  • Unsteady walking
  • New balance problems

When to Seek Help

  • You fall more than once
  • You feel unsteady when walking
  • You have dizziness or fainting spells
  • You are afraid of falling
