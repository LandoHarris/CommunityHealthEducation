Tracking Your Vitals

Why Tracking Vitals Matters

Keeping track of your vital signs helps you understand your health, manage chronic conditions, and catch problems early.

  • Shows how your body is responding to treatment
  • Helps your doctor make better decisions
  • Can prevent serious complications

Important Vitals to Track

  • Blood Pressure — measures heart and artery health
  • Blood Sugar — important for diabetes management
  • Heart Rate — shows how hard your heart is working
  • Weight — helps track fluid retention and overall health
  • Oxygen Level (SpO₂) — important for lung conditions

How to Track Blood Pressure

  • Sit quietly for 5 minutes before measuring
  • Keep feet flat on the floor
  • Place cuff on bare upper arm
  • Measure at the same time each day
  • Record your readings

Learn more about blood pressure

How to Track Blood Sugar

  • Wash hands before testing
  • Use a clean lancet each time
  • Follow your doctor’s testing schedule
  • Record your numbers after each test

Learn more about diabetes management

Using a Health Log

Write down your vitals daily or use a phone app. Include:

  • Date and time
  • Your readings
  • How you feel
  • Any symptoms

When to Call Your Doctor

  • Blood pressure is very high or very low
  • Blood sugar stays above your target range
  • You feel dizzy, weak, or short of breath
  • Your oxygen level drops below 92%
