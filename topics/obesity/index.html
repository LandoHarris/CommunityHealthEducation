Obesity & Healthy Weight

What Is Obesity?

Obesity means having too much body fat. It increases the risk of many health problems, including diabetes, heart disease, and high blood pressure.

Reaching and maintaining a healthy weight can greatly improve your overall health.

Health Risks of Obesity

  • Type 2 diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Heart disease
  • Stroke
  • Sleep apnea
  • Joint pain
  • Certain cancers

Healthy Ways to Lose Weight

  • Eat more fruits and vegetables
  • Choose whole grains
  • Limit sugary drinks and snacks
  • Exercise regularly
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Get enough sleep

Setting Realistic Goals

Small changes make a big difference. Losing even 5–10% of your body weight can improve your health.

  • Start with simple goals
  • Track your progress
  • Celebrate small wins

When to Talk to Your Doctor

  • You’re gaining weight quickly
  • You have trouble losing weight
  • You have symptoms of diabetes or high blood pressure
  • You want help creating a weight‑loss plan
