Healthy Eating

Why Healthy Eating Matters

Eating well helps you maintain a healthy weight, manage chronic conditions, and feel your best every day.

  • Improves energy levels
  • Supports heart health
  • Helps control blood sugar
  • Strengthens your immune system

Healthy Food Choices

  • Fruits and vegetables
  • Whole grains (brown rice, oats, whole wheat bread)
  • Lean proteins (chicken, fish, beans)
  • Low‑fat dairy
  • Healthy fats (olive oil, nuts)

Foods to Limit

  • Sugary drinks
  • Fried foods
  • Processed snacks
  • High‑salt foods
  • Excess red meat

Tips for Eating Healthy on a Budget

  • Buy frozen fruits and vegetables
  • Choose store brands
  • Plan meals ahead of time
  • Cook at home instead of eating out
  • Use coupons and weekly sales

Healthy Plate Method

Use this simple method to build balanced meals:

  • ½ plate fruits and vegetables
  • ¼ plate lean protein
  • ¼ plate whole grains

When to Seek Help

  • You’re losing or gaining weight unexpectedly
  • You have trouble affording healthy foods
  • You need help managing diabetes or high blood pressure
  • You want personalized nutrition advice
