Mental Health Support

Why Mental Health Matters

Mental health affects how you think, feel, and handle stress. It is just as important as physical health, especially when managing chronic conditions.

Common Mental Health Challenges

  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • Stress and burnout
  • Loneliness or isolation
  • Grief and loss

Signs You May Need Support

  • Feeling sad or hopeless most days
  • Loss of interest in activities
  • Changes in sleep or appetite
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Feeling overwhelmed or constantly worried

Ways to Improve Mental Well‑Being

  • Talk to someone you trust
  • Stay active and exercise regularly
  • Eat balanced meals
  • Get enough sleep
  • Practice deep breathing or relaxation
  • Spend time outdoors

When to Seek Professional Help

  • Your symptoms last more than two weeks
  • You feel unable to cope with daily life
  • You have severe anxiety or panic attacks
  • You feel disconnected from others
  • You have thoughts of harming yourself

Talking with a doctor or counselor can help you feel supported and understood.

Supporting Someone Else

  • Listen without judgment
  • Encourage them to talk to a professional
  • Check in regularly
  • Help with daily tasks if needed
  • Be patient and understanding
