Exercise Recommendations

Why Exercise Matters

Regular physical activity:

  • Strengthens your heart and lungs
  • Helps control weight
  • Lowers blood pressure and blood sugar
  • Improves mood and mental health
  • Reduces risk of disease
  • Improves balance and flexibility
  • Keeps muscles and bones strong

How Much Exercise Do You Need?

Adults (18–64 years)

  • Aerobic activity: 150 minutes of moderate activity per week
  • OR: 75 minutes of vigorous activity per week
  • Strength training: 2 days per week

Older Adults (65+ years)

  • Aerobic activity: 150 minutes per week
  • Strength training: 2 days per week
  • Balance exercises: 3+ days per week

Types of Exercise

Aerobic (Cardio)

Gets your heart pumping:

  • Brisk walking
  • Jogging or running
  • Dancing
  • Swimming
  • Cycling
  • Sports

Strength Training

Builds and maintains muscle:

  • Weight lifting
  • Resistance bands
  • Push-ups and sit-ups
  • Yoga
  • Heavy gardening

Flexibility

Improves range of motion:

  • Stretching
  • Yoga
  • Tai chi
  • Pilates

Balance

Helps prevent falls:

  • Tai chi
  • Balance exercises
  • Walking on a line
  • Yoga

Getting Started

  • Talk to your doctor first
  • Start slowly (10–15 minutes per day)
  • Increase activity gradually
  • Choose activities you enjoy
  • Exercise with a friend
  • Set realistic goals

Easy Ways to Add Activity

  • Walk instead of driving short distances
  • Garden or do yard work
  • Take stairs instead of elevators
  • Do housework
  • Dance to music
  • Play with grandchildren
  • Walk after meals
  • Join a community class

Safe Exercise Tips

  • Warm up before exercising
  • Cool down afterwards
  • Wear supportive shoes
  • Stay hydrated
  • Stop if you feel pain
  • Exercise in safe, well-lit areas

When to Stop and Rest

Stop exercising and contact your doctor if you experience:

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Severe shortness of breath
  • Dizziness or fainting
  • Severe joint or muscle pain
