Blood Pressure Management

What Is Blood Pressure?

Blood pressure is the force of blood pushing against your artery walls. High blood pressure (hypertension) can damage your heart, kidneys, and blood vessels.

Healthy Blood Pressure Range

  • Normal: Below 120/80
  • Elevated: 120–129 / below 80
  • High (Stage 1): 130–139 / 80–89
  • High (Stage 2): 140+ / 90+

How to Lower Blood Pressure

  • Eat less salt
  • Exercise regularly
  • Maintain a healthy weight
  • Limit alcohol
  • Manage stress
  • Take medications as prescribed

How to Measure Blood Pressure at Home

  • Sit quietly for 5 minutes
  • Keep feet flat on the floor
  • Place cuff on bare upper arm
  • Measure at the same time daily
  • Record your readings

When to Call Your Doctor

  • Your readings are consistently above 140/90
  • You feel dizzy or lightheaded
  • You have chest pain or shortness of breath
  • Your blood pressure suddenly spikes
