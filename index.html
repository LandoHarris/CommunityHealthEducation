Alabama Rural Health Association 

Jacksonville State University Chapter 

Our Mission

The Goal of ARHA is to improve and empower our Rural and Local Communities in various ways that benefit the health of not only the person but the health of the Community as a whole as well.

There are many ways to improve Rural or Community Health. Many of the steps towards improvement are dependant on the Community and it's needs. That being said it's is vital to listen to the needs of the Community and Understand the ways in which everyone can do their part.

Who We Are

The ARHA Chapter at JSU is one of many Chapters throughout Alabama. All of which are devoted in improving and interacting with each of their own local Communities in various ways. Each Chapter is made primarily of Students that have an interest in the Health and Well-being of the Local Community. Many of which are from the Community that they now seek to improve.
