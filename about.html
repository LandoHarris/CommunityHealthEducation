Our History

Founded with a vision to provide free, accessible health education, our organization serves as a vital resource for the local community. What began as an initiative at JSU of Alabama, founded by alumna Harli Smith, has grown into a dedicated platform for public health awareness.

Today, under the leadership of Chapter President Landon Harris—also representing the Alabama Rural Health Association—we continue to expand our reach. Our mission remains steadfast: to bridge the gap in health literacy and provide actionable, free resources for the betterment of the community.

What is ARHA?

The Alabama Rural Health Association (ARHA) is the leading voice for rural health in Alabama. By connecting professionals, students, and community members, ARHA works to improve the quality of health services and expand access to care across the state's rural landscapes.

As a JSU chapter, we align our local education efforts with the broader state-wide goals of ARHA, ensuring our resources are evidence-based and tailored to the unique needs of our community. Learn more at the ARHA website →
