About Us

Learn about our mission and the team behind Community Health Education.

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to reliable health information. Our organization provides free, evidence-based health education to underserved communities, helping people make informed decisions about their wellbeing.

Our History

Founded in 2015, Community Health Education started as a small volunteer group hosting monthly workshops at the local library. Today, we serve thousands of community members through in-person events and online resources.
