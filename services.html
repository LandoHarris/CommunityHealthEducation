Our Services

Free programs and resources for community members.

🫀 Heart Health Program

Weekly sessions covering cardiovascular health, blood pressure management, and heart-healthy eating. Includes free blood pressure checks.

When: Every Tuesday, 6:00 PM

🧠 Mental Wellness Workshops

Learn stress management techniques, mindfulness practices, and when to seek professional help. Safe, judgment-free environment.

When: First Saturday of each month, 10:00 AM

🥗 Nutrition & Cooking Classes

Budget-friendly healthy cooking demonstrations. Take home recipes and learn to read nutrition labels.

When: Every Thursday, 5:30 PM

💉 Vaccination Clinics

Free flu shots and routine vaccinations in partnership with the county health department.

When: Seasonal — check calendar for dates
