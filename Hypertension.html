Who Does Hypertension Affect?

Young Adults

Often overlooked, but about 1 in 5 are affected. Early awareness can prevent long-term heart disease.

Middle-Aged Adults

Risk increases with lifestyle and aging. Many are diagnosed, but control remains a challenge.

Older Adults

Most affected group. Blood pressure becomes harder to manage due to aging and chronic conditions.

Why Hypertension Matters

Hypertension is one of the most preventable health conditions, yet it remains a leading cause of heart disease, stroke, and kidney problems. It often has no symptoms, which makes awareness and regular monitoring critical.

The effects extend beyond individuals, impacting families, healthcare systems, and communities. Early prevention and management can significantly reduce long-term health risks and improve quality of life.
